Share your taxi

Passengers near you can see your taxi on the map and find you faster. Free to use.

Passengers use it to spot your taxi. Example: HIM 007 GP
  1. Type your number plate.
  2. Tap Start sharing and allow location.
  3. Leave this page open on your phone holder. If you switch to another app, passengers see your taxi as "last seen" until you come back.
